What it does

Everything the operator needs on one screen.

Command center

Live occupancy, today’s revenue, kitchen SLA, arrivals & departures and delayed-order alerts — the whole hotel at a glance.

Restaurant floor

A visual dining floor. Tap a table, place an order and it reaches the kitchen in seconds. Quick billing the moment the guest asks.

Kitchen queue (KDS)

Live tickets by status with opening → preparing → ready → served. Ready/served pings and delay warnings at 15 minutes.

Rooms & stays

Room grid, check-in/check-out, bookings, room-service orders and folio billing — everything follows the guest, nothing lost at checkout.

Billing & GST

CGST+SGST split invoices for F&B (5%) and rooms (12–18%), room-folio charging, daily & monthly audit, sales invoice register for your CA.

Inventory

Suppliers, purchase bills, stock levels, kitchen usage and low-stock alerts — know what’s in the store, with CSV import.

Security & audit

Role-based staff access with PIN + password, supervisor approvals for overrides, and a complete audit trail of every action.
