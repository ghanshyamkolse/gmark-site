# GMARK — 5-Minute Demo Script (seeded demo property)

Target: hotel/restaurant owner or manager. Goal: show the three things they
care about — *(a) I can run today's business from one screen, (b) kitchen
communication is no longer shouting, (c) my bills and taxes are sorted.*

Set-up beforehand: installed app, logged in as `admin@example.com` /
`changeme`. The seed creates a working demo hotel (rooms, menu, busy tables,
live kitchen orders, settled bills).

---

## :00 — Hook (30 seconds)

> "This runs on one PC at the property — no internet needed, your data never
> leaves the building."

Show the login screen → log in as manager. Point at the **Command center**
and read three numbers aloud: occupancy %, today's revenue, and open kitchen
orders. Say: *"This is the whole hotel at a glance."*

## :30 — Dining floor (1.5 minutes)

Open **Restaurant floor**.
- Click the table marked **T01** (already has an OPEN order).
- Show the order items and total in the detail panel.
- Click **Place order** → tap `Paneer Tikka` twice → **Send order to kitchen**.
- Say: *"From here to the kitchen in under five seconds. No paper, no
  misreading handwriting."*

## 1:45 — Kitchen queue (1 minute)

Open **Kitchen queue**.
- Show the new `Paneer Tikka` order appear as a ticket.
- Click **Ready** on it (or on an existing READY one) → the notice chimes and
  the floor marks the table "Ready to serve".
- Say: *"The captain sees 'ready to serve' on the floor without walking back."*

## 2:30 — Rooms & folio (1 minute)

Open **Rooms & stays**.
- Show room 102 occupied (guest Vikram Mehta) and the room-service order on
  room 106.
- On 106, open the room-service line and the bill preview.
- Say: *"Room-service food lands on the guest's folio automatically — nothing
  gets lost at checkout."*

## 3:15 — Billing & GST (1 minute)

Open **Restaurant floor → Table checkout**, pick an order, Review bill.
- Show the CGST+SGST split and payment method (Cash/UPI/Card/Room folio).
- Print the receipt to the preview.
- Open **Finance** → show the daily audit table and today's settled invoices.
- Say: *"Every bill is a tax-valid invoice and your nightly register is one
  button away."*

## 4:15 — Close (45 seconds)

- Mention the 30-day trial (seeded demo included), one-time license, and the
  included setup/training day.
- Ask the money question: *"What does your current billing take you per month —
  software fees, and time spent on GST at month-end?"*
- Offer next step: full training day on a real menu/room list, or run it in
  parallel for a fortnight.
